## Scope of release

The included MIT licence is for original source **code only**. It does not override the ODbL 1.0 or CC BY 4.0 terms of third-party geographic data. Public per-building tables and GIS intermediates are **not redistributed in this code-only package**. If they are later published, the dataset release must have separately reviewed licensing, metadata, attributions, and (where applicable) ODbL compliance.
